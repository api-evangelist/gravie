By Mimi Sibley – SVP & General Manager, Gravie ICHRA The ICHRA market is growing up fast, and the conversation around it hasn’t caught up. There are now more than 50 ICHRA administrators in the market, with private equity and venture capital pouring in. Companies like HealthSherpa and ICHRAx are building the underlying rails.
