Individual Coverage Health Reimbursement Arrangements (ICHRAs) experienced gangbuster growth in 2025. According to the HRA Council, ICHRAs grew 34% among large employers and 52% among other employer types. It’s no wonder that Congress has started paying attention to ICHRAs for the first time in several years.
